## Quick reference — adult attachment (Bartholomew 4-style)

| Style | Self-model | Other-model | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Secure** | Positive | Positive | Comfortable with intimacy and autonomy |
| **Preoccupied** | Negative | Positive | Clingy, hyperactivating strategies |
| **Dismissing-Avoidant** | Positive | Negative | Deactivating strategies, minimizes intimacy |
| **Fearful-Avoidant** | Negative | Negative | Disorganized, hot-and-cold |
