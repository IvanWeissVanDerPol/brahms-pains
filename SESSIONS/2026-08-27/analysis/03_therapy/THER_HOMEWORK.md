## Iván — 3 experiments

### 1. Practice saying no (3 small no's)
**What:** Three small denials this week.
- Example: "No, I can't drive you to Biggie right now."
- Example: "No, I'm not available for that meeting."
- Example: "No, I need to be alone tonight."

**Why:** The "no puedo decir que no" pattern runs Iván. **Small no's start to break it.**

**Track:** Write each one down. Notice the resistance. Notice the consequences (likely none).

### 2. Name an affect without translating it (1 per day)
**What:** Once per day, name a feeling without going to English or to clinical language.
- Example: "I am sad" — not "I'm feeling down today"
- Example: "I am scared" — not "the situation is uncertain"
- Example: "I am angry" — not "the thing is suboptimal"

**Why:** Iván translates affect to concept. **Naming without translating** is the practice.

**Track:** Notice when translation kicks in. Notice what happens when you don't translate.

### 3. Tell the cancer scare story in full
**What:** Write or tell one person the full cancer scare story. Don't summarize. Don't drop it. Include the affect.

**Why:** The room dropped this moment. **Naming it again** brings it back.

**Track:** Notice how it feels to tell it fully.

**Why:** DBT skills are evidence-based for TLP-pattern behaviors. **Using one** is the next step.

**Track:** Notice when you use it. Notice what happens.
