# Individual Therapy Plan — 8 Weeks per Speaker

**Frame:** Concrete, actionable. Each speaker has **8 weeks of individual work** to do alongside group work.

**Disclaimer:** Not a clinical prescription. Each plan assumes the speaker will work with **their own qualified clinician** — these are **topics to bring** and **experiments to try**, not treatment protocols.

---

## Iván — 8-week individual plan

### Theme: "From host to person"
**The core work:** Iván needs to stop hosting his own affect.

### Weekly topics
| Week | Topic | Experiment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Origin family inventory** | What's the deferred material? Write 1 page about origin family |
| 2 | **The "no puedo decir que no" pattern** | Practice one small "no" per day. Track in a journal |
| 3 | **Cancer scare return** | Write the full story. What was the affect you dropped? |
| 4 | **The hub exhaustion** | Notice when you're hosting. Notice when you're not. Log |
| 5 | **Affect exposure** | Name a feeling before translating it. Daily practice |
| 6 | **The polycule as family-of-choice** | What does it give you? What does it cost? |
| 7 | **Reciprocity** | Ask for one thing from one person per week |
| 8 | **Re-narrate** | How has your story changed in 8 weeks? |

### Indicators of progress
- Can name a feeling without translating it
- Can say no to a small request without guilt
- Can ask for help directly

### Indicators of stuckness
- Still hosting in every conversation
- Still deferring own affect
- Still no reciprocal asks

## Gaby — 8-week individual plan

### Theme: "From caregiver to cared-for"
**The core work:** Gaby needs to receive care as much as she gives it.

### Weekly topics
| Week | Topic | Experiment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The father's death** | Write the full story. Don't edit. |
| 2 | **The self-blame** | Notice the "could I have saved him?" loop. Write a letter to him |
| 3 | **The autism disclosure** | Find a specialist in adult diagnosis. Schedule an assessment |
| 4 | **The caregiving compulsion** | When did it start? Trace it back |
| 5 | **Receive care** | Accept one offer of help per week. Don't reciprocate immediately |
| 6 | **"Mi mujer"** | Bring her into the conversation. What's her role in all this? |
| 7 | **RCP and the saving pattern** | What's the connection between RCP and the father's death? |
| 8 | **Re-narrate** | How has the caregiving compulsion changed? |

### Indicators of progress
- Can receive care without reciprocating
- Can name her own needs
- Has processed the father's death affect

### Indicators of stuckness
- Still showing up at the hospital when not asked
- Still doing all the household labor
- Still self-blame about the father's death

### Specialty referrals needed
1. **Adult autism specialist** — for the disclosure
2. **Prolonged grief therapist** — for the father's death
3. **Trauma-informed therapist** — for the cumulative ACE pattern

## Cookie/Kuki — 8-week plan (if she's in next session)

### Theme: "From acting-out to mentalization"
**The core work:** Cookie/Kuki needs to build a structure she can live inside.

### Weekly topics
| Week | Topic | Experiment |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Safety planning** | Written. Concrete. With contact authorities trigger |
| 2 | **The TLP label** | What does it mean to you? What does it give? What does it cost? |
| 3 | **The daughter's welfare** | First question: what does she need? |
| 4 | **The 80 calls** | What's the alternative? Practice one call per day instead |
| 5 | **DBT skills** | Distress tolerance + emotion regulation |
| 6 | **The bed incident** | Process the boundary violation with a clinician |
| 7 | **Mentalization** | Hold your own mind + others' minds as separate |
| 8 | **Re-narrate** | What's different? |

### Indicators of progress
- Has a written safety plan
- Can call once per day instead of 80 times
- Can name what the daughter needs

### Indicators of stuckness
- Continues 80 calls
- Daughter welfare unclear
- Self-harm or harm to others

### Specialty referrals needed
1. **DBT program** — full skills group + individual therapy
2. **Mentalization-based therapy** (Bateman/Fonagy)
3. **Child welfare assessment** — for the daughter
4. **Trauma-focused therapy** — EMDR or somatic experiencing

## Cross-cutting weekly practice

**For everyone in the household:**

1. **One small denial** — practice saying no
2. **One small receipt** — practice receiving
3. **One small naming** — name an affect before translating it

These three practices, done weekly for 8 weeks, would **change the household dynamic** more than any session-level intervention.
