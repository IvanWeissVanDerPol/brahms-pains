## The TLP label question

**The room uses the TLP label.** Lourdes says TLP. Cuqui says TLP. Manuel disagrees.

**Why this matters:**
- TLP/BPD has evidence-based treatments (DBT, MBT, TFP)
- TLP can also be **misdiagnosed** — C-PTSD, bipolar, ADHD can all look similar
- The label does **work** in the room — gives a shared framework
- The label also **displaces** — bed incident becomes "symptom" rather than "relational event"

**Recommended:**
1. **Differential assessment** — ITQ (International Trauma Questionnaire) to distinguish BPD from C-PTSD
2. **Don't over-rely on the label** — use it as a framework, not a destination
3. **Treat the behavior, not the label** — the 80 calls need a plan, regardless of diagnosis
4. **If Lourdes is still treating** — she should consider whether the dual role is appropriate
