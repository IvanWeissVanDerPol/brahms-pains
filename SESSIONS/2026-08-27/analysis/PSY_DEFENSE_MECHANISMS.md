# Defense Mechanisms Inventory — 2026-08-27 Group

**Frame:** Vaillant's hierarchy (1977) ranks defenses from immature (psychotic) to mature. Anna Freud's original taxonomy. McWilliams' "Psychoanalytic Case Formulation" (1999) for narrative defenses.

**Note:** This is **observed-behavior mapping**, not diagnosis. Defenses are normal; it's the **flexibility and context-sensitivity** that matters.

---

## Vaillant's hierarchy (for reference)

| Level | Defenses | Adaptive cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Mature** | Sublimation, suppression, altruism, humor, anticipation | Low |
| **Neurotic** | Repression, displacement, intellectualization, reaction formation, isolation of affect | Medium |
| **Immature** | Projection, fantasy, denial, splitting, acting out, passive aggression, projective identification | High |
| **Psychotic** | Distortion, delusional projection, denial of external reality | Disabling |

## Group-level defense pattern

The group as a whole uses:
- **Splitting** — Cuqui is simultaneously the assailant, the patient, and the daughter
- **Denial** — Nobody addressed the MRI scare, the household exclusion, or Lourdes' 2-year cost
- **Projective identification** — The room **displaces** its own "too nice" pattern onto Cuqui
- **Acting out** — The bed incident, the 80 calls, the household exit were all **acted out**, not talked out
- **Sublimation** — The session itself is a **mature sublimation** of the household's distress

**The room is using session-as-defensive-structure.** That is functional in the short term and **dysfunctional in the long term** if it substitutes for individual work.

## Intervention framework

**General principle:** Identify the **primary defense**, then **gently point out where it's failing the person**.

For example:
- Iván's intellectualization fails him when he drops the cancer scare and nobody picks it up.
- Gaby's altruistic surrender fails her when she has to receive care (the hospital experience).
- Lourdes' withdrawal fails her when she realizes she's been outside for 2 years.

**In each case, the intervention is: name the defense, name its cost, offer an alternative.**
