The way I hold all three: I cannot resolve them into a single narrative. Gaby's experience is real. The touching was real. The accusation is real. They are three different events that happen to share the same bed and the same hour. Holding all three without collapsing any of them is the work.

## 🛏 THE 08-23 THREESOME — Partial-Participation-Then-Decline-Further

During the kissing, the tasting, the oral, the grinding — my body was doing what bodies do. The engagement with Nate was different from the engagement with Belén. With Nate: easy, familiar, light. ### The retroactive "you should have asked more"

The moving goalpost. **The pattern is identical: act in good faith, then be told after the fact that the act was insufficient.**

The thing I notice now, writing this: the pattern only works if I agree I was wrong. If I hold that I was present, engaged, and then declined because I was tired — and the household norm says that engagement + decline = insufficient consent — then the household has no protocol for "I'm tired, I'm stopping." **The retroactive norm erases my right to decline in the moment.** That's the deepest thing about it.
