# Evidence Ledger — Core Wounds

> **Scope:** Messaging and voice-note evidence that supports or challenges existing wound formulations.  
> **Guardrail:** Early-childhood origin claims rely partly on self-report preserved in repository documents; the corpus cannot independently verify age, causality, or diagnosis.

## 1. “Pesado” / being too much

**Evidence in present-day communication**

- Ideline: _“Sorry if I'm too random sometimes jaja”_ (2023-01-30).
- Across chats, `COMMUNICATION_PATTERNS_ANALYSIS.md` identifies apologies before requests, request hedging, passive waiting, and `uwu` as a vulnerability softener.
- Voice-note analysis reports **353 self-deprecation** candidates in six relationships; the broader extraction contains **286** candidates.
- `PTT-20250730-WA0012.opus`: _“yo acepté lo que jamás sería good enough.”_

**Counterevidence / healing**

- Alex/Sarah samples contain zero “pesado” apologies, direct requests, explicit wants and reciprocal pleasure.
- Cookie shows direct asking is possible in a bounded intellectual context.
- The existing Fixer profile records Ivan’s correction that the “service = right to exist” framing rates only 1/10 for him; self-confidence and control are also part of the pattern.

**Working meaning**

The strongest defensible claim is not “Ivan believes he should not exist.” It is that some vulnerable requests are pre-softened to reduce the risk of being experienced as demanding.

## 2. Emotional exposure becoming unsafe

**Evidence**

- `MASTER_PROFILE.md` summarizes: _“mi vieja nunca me escuchaba, mi viejo es siempre de mandar”_ (`PTT-20250729-WA0012.opus`).
- Family remains central yet is described as receiving less emotional detail than selected friends, partners or the private voice journal.
- The Firewall pattern is therefore supported behaviorally even when the exact childhood origin cannot be established from Messaging alone.

**Limit**

The “Lipstick Incident” and its causal meaning are autobiographical claims. Keep the narrative, but distinguish remembered event, current interpretation and corpus-observed behavior.

## 3. Unavailable or inconsistent care

**Evidence**

- The Laura/Fistula material is the repository’s clearest adult reenactment: illness, crying alone, and experienced absence of care.
- Relationship balance analysis rates Laura as extreme imbalance and several other relationships as service-heavy.
- `CATEGORY_EXTRACTION.json` contains **510 neglect/abandonment** and **476 illness/medical** keyword candidates requiring contextual validation.
- The master profile records the desired care response: _“si me siento sad, esté así para mí, me abrace de atrás… y quede calentito”_ (`PTT-20250729-WA0046.opus`).

**Counterevidence**

- Historical receiving-care examples include Lujan, Lucy, Jessica and Ideline.
- Current secure counterexamples include Nico, Mike, Alex and Sarah.

## 4. Touch deprivation / warmth hunger

**Evidence**

- `CATEGORY_EXTRACTION.json`: **1,145 physical-affection** and **59 physical-needs** candidates.
- `PTT-20250813-WA0003.opus`: preference for physical touch and comfort — _“abrazarme… darme besitos.”_
- Nico, 2026-01-12: _“me re subió los ánimos los cuddles y las cuerdas.”_
- Nico, 2026-01-23: _“Def es re nice sentir el warmth y care.”_
- The master profile records improved neck/body state after touch: _“mi cuello está más happy… me sentí warm con touch”_ (2026-01-27).

**Updated formulation**

“Touch starvation” is better framed as a recurring unmet or inconsistently met need, not a permanent state. January 2026 evidence shows active repair and embodied integration.

## 5. Shame and identity integration around HIV

**Evidence**

- `NEW_INSIGHTS_JANUARY_2026.md` contrasts earlier secrecy/shame with proactive, educational status disclosure in the Alex relationship.
- The same analysis finds no shame language in the disclosure, explicit U=U discussion and planned monitoring.

**Interpretation**

This is evidence of an identity wound moving toward integration. It should not be collapsed into attachment or sexual pathology; disclosure competence, consent and health advocacy are strengths.

## Cross-wound chain

```text
Fear of being too much
→ soften the request / hide the need
→ give service instead
→ care remains implicit or unreciprocated
→ loneliness, resentment or shutdown
```

The corpus also shows the reverse chain:

```text
Explicit permission + consent + reciprocity
→ direct request
→ care received and named in the body
→ gratitude and continued openness
```
