# Evidence Ledger — Attachment Patterns

> **Scope:** Messaging messages and voice-note transcripts only.  
> **Interpretive status:** Pattern hypotheses, not diagnoses. Quotes may contain Whisper errors; the file/date reference is the audit trail.

## Corpus-level anchors

- `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/VOICE_NOTE_ANALYSIS.md` reports **2,497 needs-expression**, **1,685 vulnerability**, and **604 deflection/minimizing** matches across six high-signal relationships.
- `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/CATEGORY_EXTRACTION.json` contains **1,145 physical-affection**, **59 physical-needs**, **510 neglect/abandonment**, **206 asking-for-help**, and **525 wants/needs** matches.
- The relationship corpus contains secure counterexamples; attachment behavior changes by context rather than forming one fixed style.

## Pattern 1 — Family proximity with emotional filtering

**Observed evidence**

- The family remains structurally central: five family members appear in the close circle described in `PSYCHOLOGICAL_ANALYSIS_20HATS.md`.
- The existing master synthesis describes family attachment as **dismissive-avoidant / Firewall**: logistics and competence are shareable, vulnerable affect is filtered (`SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/MASTER_PROFILE.md`, Attachment Patterns section).
- Voice evidence: `PTT-20250729-WA0012.opus` is summarized in the master profile as: _“mi vieja nunca me escuchaba, mi viejo es siempre de mandar.”_

**Interpretation**

Proximity does not equal emotional openness. The data supports a split between dependable family access and guarded emotional disclosure. This is better described as a contextual strategy than a global attachment label.

**Counterevidence / limits**

- High and recent family contact is a secure resource, not evidence of dysfunction by itself.
- Messaging cannot establish childhood attachment classification or the internal quality of offline interactions.

## Pattern 2 — Anxious edge around intimate need

**Observed evidence**

- `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/COMMUNICATION_PATTERNS_ANALYSIS.md` documents pre-emptive apologies: _“Sorry if I'm too random sometimes jaja”_ (Ideline, 2023-01-30), request hedging, and `uwu` as a softener.
- The same report identifies a central asymmetry: Ivan can give crisis care and practical help, but emotional asking is rarely observed without a permission structure.
- `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/MASTER_PROFILE.md` records the desired response: _“abrazarme si me está viendo, darme besitos”_ (`PTT-20250813-WA0003.opus`).
- During illness in the Laura relationship, the recorded “Fistula Incident” is treated across the repository as a concrete attachment injury: need was high and care received was experienced as absent.

**Interpretation**

The anxious component appears less as overt pursuit and more as suppression, monitoring, indirectness, and fear of becoming burdensome. Need is present; direct bids for care are constrained.

## Pattern 3 — Earned security through explicit permission

**Observed evidence**

- `SOURCE_OF_TRUTH/NEW_INSIGHTS_JANUARY_2026.md` documents direct requests, immediate appreciation, ongoing consent check-ins, and zero “pesado” apologies in the Alex/Sarah samples.
- Quotes include: _“A mi me gustaría practicar más pedir que quiero”_; _“me preguntabas si estaba bien cada paso uwu”_; and _“me sentí warm y fuzzy.”_
- The Nico material records: _“Estaba en un modo sad… me re subió los ánimos los cuddles y las cuerdas”_ (2026-01-12) and _“Def es re nice sentir el warmth y care”_ (2026-01-23).
- Cookie provides a lower-intensity counterexample: Ivan directly asks for research help, showing that asking capacity exists when stakes are bounded and intellectual.

**Interpretation**

Clear roles, consent, predictability, and reciprocal acknowledgment reduce attachment threat. These contexts are evidence of **state-dependent secure functioning** and a pathway toward broader earned security.

## Working formulation

- **Family:** high structural closeness + selective emotional avoidance.
- **Romantic/intimate:** secure capacity with an anxious edge around being “too much.”
- **Kink/explicit-consent contexts:** strongest directness, receiving, and embodied safety.
- **Balanced friendships:** proof that reciprocity and asking are possible outside intimacy.

## Testable questions

1. Does direct emotional asking become easier with the same people over time, or only inside named roles?
2. What happens after a care bid is received well: relief, disbelief, escalation, or withdrawal?
3. Are late-night voice notes replacing bids to humans, or simply organizing thoughts before a human conversation?
4. Which family member, if any, receives vulnerable content without Firewall filtering?
