It is false to frame this as Ivan failing to love. **He loves enormously; he expresses through DOING and BEING, not through casual verbal declarations.** The fistful of declarations that do exist are concentrated at exactly the moments they need to be — that's not deficit, that's *precision*.

4. **The 1.0 balanced ratio with Ale (Ivan 2, Ale 2) was celebrated as "the only balanced relationship."** That's still meaningful — but the interpretation shifts: it's not that they're "balanced in love," it's that their friendship has reached the equilibrium where both parties express at the same frequency because their relational rhythm is matched. **Other relationships have different rhythms; that's not imbalance.**
