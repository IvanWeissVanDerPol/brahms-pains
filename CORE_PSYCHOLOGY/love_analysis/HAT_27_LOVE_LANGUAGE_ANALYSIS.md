## 🎯 TL;DR — The Seven Findings

1. **Ivan is a WORDS-DOMINANT lover** — 2,415 explicit verbal-affection markers across 519 chats. Words of affirmation is his primary love language in text, especially with **family and ex-partners**.
2. **TOUCH is the second modality** — 1,642 markers. It's dominant in **kink/intimate contexts** (Lourdes, Somosgay, Jonatan, Gabriella, Thais) but absent in family.
3. **ACTS-OF-SERVICE is low (696 markers)** despite being his **most reliable mode of caring** — he shows love through doing (preparing, fixing, sending, organizing) but rarely narrates it as such.
4. **GIFTS are minimal (102 markers)** — sharing memes/links counts as a proxy gift; physical gifting is rare.
5. **REPAIR language is huge for him (875 markers)** — Ivan initiates 48.4% of conversation repairs across all chats. He's a **rebuilder**, not an avoider.
6. **SHADOW LANGUAGE is the diagnostic finding** — 39+ explicit moments of "no me sale / no sé expresar / me cuesta". This is the cost of being an autistic person raised in a non-affirming family. **The love is there; the bridge to expression is broken.**
7. **24.1% of touch markers happen late-night (22:00-04:00 PY)** vs his 32% baseline for all messaging — touch is slightly *less* concentrated at night than his other modalities. **His most intimate signals land during the day, when he's regulated.**

## 🩺 Clinical Synthesis

### How Ivan Loves: A Composite

**Ivan loves by DOING.** He sends study material, he builds websites, he sends money, he fixes code, he plans trips. This is the **Fixer** defense operating as his primary love channel. It feels safe because it's competent, controlled, and doesn't require verbal vulnerability.

**Ivan loves by SAYING (lightly).** "Linda", "guapa", "lindo", "te quiero" — all in casual, surface-affection registers. He says these freely with family, friends, and ex. He does NOT say "te amo" or "I love you" easily; the deeper verbal register is gated by the Firewall.

**Ivan loves by REPAIRING.** When he ruptures, he repairs — sometimes excessively (48.4% of conversation repairs initiated by him). This is love expressed through the act of coming back.

**Ivan loves by TOUCHING (in permitted spaces only).** Kink contexts, Jonatan, Somosgay, Thais, Gaby — these are the people who have given him explicit permission to reference touch, cuddles, "mimitos". **Family almost never receives touch-language from Ivan.**

**Ivan cannot love by DECLARING.** Shadow language — 39 markers — show him failing at the very thing he most needs to do. "No me sale" is the recurring phrase. This is the autistic-expressive deficit + the family-of-origin wound combined.

### The Wound Behind the Pattern

Ivan cannot express the depth of love he feels because:

1. **Family punished expression early** ("cuando expresaba cosas en mi familia me retaban nomás")
2. **His father modeled non-presence** ("el mundo de su pc, el super, el patio, nada más le importa")
3. **His mother is unpredictable** (the Lipstick Incident → Firewall learned)
4. **He is autistic** — expressive language is a known deficit; he can intellectually know what he feels but not verbalize it in real-time
5. **"Pesado" labeling** — he's been told he's "too much" so he preemptively suppresses the deep channels

The result is a man who **loves enormously and shows it through the safest available channels**: doing, light words, repair. He **longs to be loved through the channels he can't access**: touch, presence, holding.

## 🎯 Practical Implications

### For Ivan himself (clinical targets)

1. **Name what he does.** He gives acts of service + repair; he should narrate these to recipients. "I'm building you this because I love you" — not just shipping the artifact.
2. **Permission structures work.** Kink + Jonatan unlock touch language. Build non-kink permission structures for touch vocabulary with close friends.
3. **Shadow language is data, not failure.** Every "no me sale" is a signal about where the wound lives. These moments are gold for therapy, not shame.
4. **Late-night touch-asking is the wound peaking.** If sleep hygiene + somatic alternatives reduce this, the touch-starvation signal will move out of the night.

### For partners

1. **Ivan will NOT ask for what he needs.** He will build you a website, not say "hold me". Watch what he *gives* — that's his love — and reciprocate in the channels he *receives* (touch, presence, comfort when sick).
2. **The fistula incident is the template.** When Ivan is sick, presence matters more than gifts. **Be there. Touch him.** That is the love language he most needs.
3. **He will say "te quiero" lightly. He will rarely say "te amo".** That gap is the wound, not the relationship.
4. **He repairs; that is love.** If you rupture and Ivan chases you back, that's not anxiety — that's his primary love channel working.

### For therapist

1. **The Shadow lexicon is a clinical goldmine.** 39 markers = 39 moments where the patient *spontaneously names his own deficit*. Use these in session.
2. **Track repair behavior.** 48.4% Ivan-initiated repair is high. Cross-reference with abandoned contacts (13) — does he repair with everyone, or only when he's the one who ruptured?
3. **The asymmetric love map (touch-in / acts-out)** is a treatment target. Teach Ivan to translate his doing-language into verbal asks.
4. **The Magali shadow confession (March 2023 + July 2025)** is the closest Ivan has come to naming the wound directly. Return to it.

## 📈 Methodology & Limitations

### What this analysis CAN tell us
- **Empirical distribution** of how Ivan expresses love across 7 modalities
- **Per-contact dominant language** — who receives what kind of love from Ivan
- **Late-night vs daytime** distribution — when his most intimate signals land
- **The shadow pattern** — where love fails to find expression
- **Stated vs demonstrated mismatch** — the central clinical finding

### What this analysis CANNOT tell us
- **What Ivan was feeling that he didn't express at all.** Shadow language only catches the moments he tried to articulate and failed. The bigger reservoir of unexpressed love is invisible.
- **Non-text modalities.** Eye contact, body language, in-room presence, tone-of-voice inflection — none captured here.
- **His side of voice notes.** VNT transcripts are speaker-agnostic; we couldn't separate Ivan's voice from the contact's. The 249-file scan is supplementary.
- **Tier 4 (group chats).** Excluded because group context dilutes personal love signals. Some intimate moments in groups are missed.

### Recommended next analyses (Hat 27, follow-up)
1. **Repair-direction asymmetry** — when Ivan is the wounded party vs the wounder, does he still repair?
2. **Shadow evolution over time** — is the "no me sale" pattern getting better or worse across 2020→2026?
3. **Gift-giving disguised as links** — count memes/articles shared to specific people as a proxy gifting analysis
4. **Family touch-language gap** — quantify the Mom/Kiki/Grandpa touch-language deficit and compare to non-family baselines
5. **Cross-reference with time_patterns.json** — Ivan's late-night touch signals vs the 32% late-night baseline, broken out by relationship type
