[2023-12-06T19:55:18] Ivan: Ingredients:

For the Filling:

250g carnaza segunda (stewing beef), diced into small pieces
1 small onion, finely chopped
2 cloves garlic, minced
2 bay leaves
1/2 teaspoon ground nutmeg
Salt and pepper to taste
2 cups beef or vegetable broth (for simmering the meat)
2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
2 tablespoons butter
Fresh parsley, finely chopped (for flavor and garnish)
Cooking oil for frying
For the Bitterballen Coating:

2-3 eggs, beaten
Bread crumbs (panko or regular)
Salt and pepper to taste
Instructions:

In a large pot, heat a little oil over medium heat. Add the diced carnaza segunda and cook until it's browned on all sides. Remove the meat from the pot and set it aside.

In the same pot, add a bit more oil if needed and sauté the chopped onions and minced garlic until they become translucent.

Return the browned meat to the pot, add bay leaves, and season with salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Stir well.

Pour in the beef or vegetable broth to cover the meat. Bring it to a simmer, then reduce the heat to low. Cover and let it cook for 1 to 1.5 hours or until the meat becomes tender and easily shredded.

Once the meat is tender, remove it from the broth and shred it finely.

In a separate pan, melt the butter over low heat, then stir in the flour to create a roux. Cook the roux for a few minutes until it becomes a light golden color.

Gradually add the cooking broth from the meat (strained to remove bay leaves) to the roux while stirring continuously to create a thick, smooth sauce. Cook for a few more minutes until the sauce thickens.

Combine the shredded meat and the sauce in a mixing bowl. Add fresh chopped parsley for flavor and mix well. Taste and adjust the seasoning if necessary.

Allow the meat mixture to cool down to room temperature, then shape it into small balls, about 1 to 1.5 inches in diameter.

Dip each ball into beaten egg, then roll them in bread crumbs seasoned with salt and pepper.

Heat oil in a deep pan or fryer to 350°F (180°C). Fry the bitterballen in batches until they turn golden brown and crispy, about 3-4 minutes per batch. Drain them on paper towels.

Serve the bitterballen hot with your choice of dipping sauce, such as mustard or a tangy dipping sauce.
