## How to use this atlas

1. **Browse** top 30 first (rich detail)
2. **Filter** by problem category for your interest area
3. **Filter** by `score_dimensions` for your priorities:
   - `faculty_match = 9` → ideal advisor in your target faculty
   - `data_availability >= 8` → minimal data-collection work
   - `novelty >= 8` → wide-open territory (high reward)
   - `advisor_activity = 10` → top-8 active advisors
   - `publication_potential >= 9` → Q1 venue
4. **Cross-check** with `academic_profiles/<surname>.json` for advisor expertise
5. **Cross-check** with `opac_una_full_v2_enriched_ckpt.json` for prior thesis density on topic
6. **Reach out** to top-3 advisors with: title + 30-sec elevator pitch + novelty bullet
