## 📋 Section 6: Clinical Implications Summary

### What This Means for Therapists

1. **Don't assume late-night messaging = stress**. Ivan's late-night is dispositional. Ask: "What part of you is messaging at 1 AM?"

2. **Don't assume high initiative = healthy**. Ivan's >70% initiator with Grandma may indicate **compulsive caretaking**, not balanced connection.

3. **Don't assume low initiative = avoidance**. Ivan's 0% in groups may be **adaptive selectivity**, not pathology.

4. **Watch for the dorsal vagal pattern**. Late-night messaging is a sign Ivan's nervous system is collapsing — he may be more vulnerable AND less rational at night.

### Recommended Session Inquiries

- "When you message at 1 AM, what part of you is speaking?"
- "Who are you pursuing? Is it obligation or desire?"
- "Why are you silent in this group? What are you protecting?"
- "If you could only message one person at 1 AM, who would it be?"
