# Doctor Visit Pack — Lateral Chest X-ray (Ivan)

> **Purpose:** make the doctor take you seriously by giving them a structured, professional package.
> Print this whole folder OR show it on a tablet. Lead with the image (annotated), not the AI text.

## Step 0 — What to bring to the appointment

```
□ Printout of xray_default.png  (the 14-bit DICOM default window — closest to what a radiologist sees on their PACS)
□ Printout of xray_annotated.png (with landmark labels)
□ This folder on USB or printed: 01_METADATA.md + 02_OBSERVATIONS.md
□ Your ID (Cedula) and the hospital patient number 396461
□ Your existing imaging CD / USB if you have one from the hospital
```

## Step 1 — Open the conversation (script)

> "Doctor, I want to be efficient with your time. I brought my own imaging from October 31. The order form for that X-ray didn't include a clinical question, so no radiologist wrote a report. I have three specific things I want addressed today, in this order. Can we work through them?"

Then **show them the annotated image**. Point at the structures. Don't say "the AI said". Say "on this image, can you tell me if the thoracic kyphosis looks exaggerated?" That puts them in the role of expert and you in the role of patient bringing data, which they like.

## Step 2 — Use the right phrase

In Paraguay/Spanish-speaking context:
- *"Necesito una segunda opinión formal sobre esta placa. ¿Puede usted darme una orden con una indicación clínica específica para que un radiólogo escriba un informe?"*
- *"Tengo molestias en [espalda alta / cuello / zona X]. Quiero descartar compromiso medular o hernia discal. ¿Me puede indicar una RM de columna [torácica / cervical / lumbar] con secuencias T1, T2 y STIR sagitales, y axiales T2 si ve algo?"*
- *"¿Puede medir el espacio retroesternal en esta placa? Si es menor de 3 cm, ¿me indica una TC de tórax con contraste?"*

## Step 3 — After the appointment

Whatever the doctor decides, **email them within 24 hours** summarizing what they said. Use this template:

```
Asunto: Confirmación de indicaciones - [fecha]

Doctor/a [nombre]:

Confirmo las indicaciones que hablamos en consulta el [fecha]:

1. [orden 1 - ej: RM columna torácica con secuencias sagital T1, T2, STIR + axial T2 si hay hallazgo]
2. [orden 2 - ej: PA tórax]
3. [orden 3 - ej: TC tórax con contraste IV]

Mi número de paciente en Centro Médico Bautista es 396461.
Estudio previo: lateral chest DX, 31-oct-2025.

Si necesita algo más antes de emitirlas, mi WhatsApp es +595 9XX XXX XXX.

Gracias,
Ivan Weiss van der Pol
```

This does **three** things doctors love:
1. Puts the orders in writing so they can't forget.
2. Gives them your prior study details so they don't waste a re-scan.
3. Shows you are an organized patient (most doctors respond faster to this).

## Step 4 — If the doctor dismisses you

It happens. Two scripts:

**Soft re-route:**
> "¿Me puede entonces derivar a un especialista? Si usted considera que esto no es su área, ¿a quién me recomienda?"

**Hard escalation (only if you sense the issue is structural — e.g., you're a 25-year-old male being dismissed because of bias):**
> "Entiendo que esta placa lateral no es diagnóstica para lo que me preocupa. Necesito que esa decisión quede registrada en mi historia clínica. Si no me indica los estudios que solicito, por favor documente el motivo en mi ficha."

In Paraguay you have the right to **copia de historia clínica** (full copy of your medical record) under Ley 6534/2020 de Atención Integral a las Personas con Discapacidad and general patient-rights norms. Use it if you need to escalate.
